### 2. Decommissioning RFC (10 min, Bill Stout)

**Decide:** drop soft mode. Recommended yes. It is the only contested part, and it is holding up a one-line change.

Filed 8/19, labelled `review`, assigned to @sarahnovotny and @imolloy, and raised at WS4 main on 8/20 ([#171](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/ws4-secure-design-agentic-systems/discussions/171) §4). Two reviews landed 8/20, and both want the stage to be terminal.

| Reviewer | Position |
|----------|----------|
| Nicolai Nielsen ([@NicolaiNielsenTR](https://github.com/NicolaiNielsenTR)) | Agrees with the idea, objects to the name. "Soft decommissioning" keeps assets and tokens and can be restarted, which is not what people hear in "decommissioned." Suggests retirement. |
| Imran Siddique ([@imran-siddique](https://github.com/imran-siddique)) | Agrees with stage 9, but would not ship soft mode. Kill switch is the control, quarantine is a restricted running state, decommissioning is the move to terminal, decommissioned is terminal. Anything reversible stays in Runtime or Maintenance. Also: revoke the credentials and tombstone the identity rather than delete it, because deleting the identifier breaks replay detection. And the teardown gate should pass when every asset found has a recorded outcome (revoked, deleted, crypto-shredded, retained until a date, held for legal, or an explicit exception), not when everything is deleted. For incident-driven retirement: quarantine, preserve evidence, revoke authority and anything downstream, dispose, record. |

Two knock-on effects if soft mode goes:

1. `riskDecommissioningReversibility` is defined *as* the soft/hard distinction, so its framing has to be rewritten before PR 3.
2. Decide whether the ADLC phase text keeps a separately named pause or quarantine state.

Also on #170: its third open question ties this to a Reflection stage ([#401](https://github.com/cosai-oasis/secure-ai-tooling/issues/401)). Recommend keeping them separate, since coupling them delays something we need this week.
